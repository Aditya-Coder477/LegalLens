# Citation & Provenance Integrity Benchmark

## 1. Overview
The Citation Verification Benchmark validates the accuracy, resolution rate, and provenance integrity of citations generated by LegalLens or cited in legal tasks.

## 2. Verification Architecture
Every legal citation (e.g. `Section 18 of SYN-ACT-005` or `SYN-ACT-001-CH-I-S01`) is checked via `CitationVerifier`:
1. **Direct Chunk Resolution**: Resolves `chunk_id` against the Knowledge Base chunks table.
2. **Statutory Hierarchy Resolution**: Decomposes citation into Document ID, Chapter, Section, Subsection, and Clause.
3. **SHA-256 Provenance Validation**: Matches the chunk content against the immutable cryptographic hash recorded during Phase 1 ingestion.
4. **Watermark / Synthetic Check**: Ensures `synthetic=True` and `source_authority=SYNTHETIC` markers are preserved.

## 3. Metrics
- **Citation Validity Rate**: $\frac{\text{Validly Resolved Citations}}{\text{Total Citations}}$ (Target: $\ge 95\%$)
- **SHA-256 Match Rate**: $\frac{\text{Hash-Verified Citations}}{\text{Valid Citations}}$ (Target: $\ge 95\%$)
- **Dead / Fabricated Citations**: Count of hallucinations claiming non-existent sections.
